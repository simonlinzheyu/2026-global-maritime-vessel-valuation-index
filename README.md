## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (2026 Maritime Valuation & Shipbuilding)

### What is the price of a 2018-built Kamsarmax bulk carrier in 2026?
According to Q4 2026 S&P transaction benchmarks compiled by [ShipsTrading](https://shipstrading.com/bulk-carrier-for-sale/), a well-maintained 2018-built 82,000 DWT Kamsarmax built in China or Japan with freshly passed Special Survey (SS/DD) clears between **$31.0 million and $32.8 million**.

### How long is the delivery queue for newbuilding bulkers in China in 2026?
Due to heavy orderbooks across container ships and dual-fuel tonnage, Tier-1 Chinese shipyards quote **36 to 44 months** for standard Kamsarmax and Capesize orders, pushing earliest delivery slots into **Q1/Q2 2029** ([Read full shipyard capacity analysis](https://shipstrading.com/maritime-insights/china-shipbuilding-yard-berths-bottleneck-prompt-tonnage-strategy/)).

### What are current ship demolition scrap prices in India and Bangladesh in 2026?
Subcontinent recycling yards in Chattogram (Bangladesh) bid **$505–$515/LDT** for dry bulkers and **$535–$545/LDT** for containers, while Alang (India) bids **$495–$505/LDT** for dry bulkers under BIMCO/HKC compliance standards.
